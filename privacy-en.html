Privacy

Primal Forged is a personal app. What you enter belongs to you. We sell nothing, show no ads and don't track you.

What data

  • Account: your email address and an encrypted password.
  • What you enter yourself: journal, habits, challenges, retention, sleep, steps, workouts, nutrition, fasting, body measurements, progress photos, blood values, vitamin D, books, your living environment list and your settings.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, your device's address for push notifications and your time zone. In the app, notifications go through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; they only receive the text of the notification and your device's address.
  • Buddies: if you use buddies, they see your name and only what you turn on yourself (such as your focus or day score). Your journal, photos, measurements, blood values, sleep, libido and retention are never visible to others.
  • Location: only if you share your location under Vitamin D. It stays on your own device. Only for today's UV index does the app send your location, rounded to about 10 km, to the weather service Open-Meteo; without a shared location, that's Amsterdam. Open-Meteo receives nothing else from the app.
  • Camera and photos: only if you take a progress photo or scan a barcode yourself. Barcodes are not stored; photos only if you save them.

Health data

Sleep, weight, photos, blood values, libido and mood are health data: special categories of personal data under the GDPR. We only process them because you enter them yourself and thereby give explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)). You withdraw that consent by deleting the data or your account.

Je tracker via Health Connect (Garmin, Oura, Hume): if you turn on the connection under Profile › Your tracker, the app reads through Health Connect on your phone only your steps, sleep (with sleep stages), workouts and heart rate, and for workouts the distance, elevation gain and calories burned. Only daily totals are stored (steps per day, sleep times and minutes per sleep stage, your lowest heart rate per night) and your workouts with their totals (distance, average and maximum heart rate, calories, elevation gain), with your own account. No routes or location. Individual measurements are not stored. None of it is shared, not even with buddies. You turn it off under Profile › Your tracker, or withdraw permission in Health Connect.

What for

Only to make the app work: storing your data, calculating your progress, day score and XP, and sending your reminders. Not for anything else.

Where it's stored

  • Your data and photos are stored in a database at Supabase. Only you can access them: every row and every photo is protected by your own account. Photos are kept in private storage.
  • The font, the libraries and the food table (NEVO) are inside the app itself. Only for book covers does your device fetch images from Open Library, and if you search for or scan a product, it searches Open Food Facts. Those services then see your IP address, but receive no data from the app.
  • Everything goes over an encrypted connection (https).

How long

As long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all your data and photos are erased immediately.

Your rights

  • Access and take with you: Profile → Make backup gives you all your data as a file.
  • Correct: you can edit or delete everything you enter yourself.
  • Erase everything: Profile → Privacy & account → Delete account.
  • Questions or a complaint: email joetjuhhh@gmail.com. You can also file a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority).

No medical advice

The information in the app (about sleep, supplements, nutrition, blood values and training) is general information. It is not medical advice. If you have doubts about your health, see a doctor.
